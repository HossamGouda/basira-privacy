Privacy Policy — Basira: Private AI Workspace

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Overview

Basira: Private AI Workspace ("Basira") is a local-first browser extension. This policy describes what data Basira handles, where it lives, and when anything leaves your device. The short version: Basira collects no analytics and stores your work in your own browser. Your content leaves your device only when you send it to the AI provider you choose (you review it before sending), when you ask for a web search (your search words go to a search engine), or when the browsing agent, in a task you started, searches Google and opens pages. Without a click, Basira makes two kinds of network request: it asks the provider you configured for its list of models, and it reads again, on a timer you can turn off, the pages you chose to watch.

What Data We Collect

Basira does not collect data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Data Basira stores locally in your browser (never on our servers — we have no servers):

  • Your provider API keys and provider preferences (stored in browser storage; you can choose key persistence per session or permanently)
  • Chat history and conversation context
  • Vault items, collections, and projects you save
  • Prompts, personas, and application preferences
  • Mind maps, scraped data, and audit results you create
  • Smart Scrape recipes (selectors and column names for a site) and keyword lists
  • Watched pages: the address, your settings for it, the text of the page as last read (to compare with), and the changes found in the last 35 days
  • Custom endpoints you save (a name, an address, a model, and a key if you enter one)
  • Models you download for on-device features

Data Basira handles transiently in memory while you work:

  • The content of pages, tabs, files, or selections you explicitly attach to a chat
  • Your open tabs list, shown to you so you can pick which tabs to attach (not retained, not transmitted)
  • Microphone audio while you dictate or record a voice note, and audio files you choose to transcribe

When Data Leaves Your Device

Basira sends your content to an AI provider only when you run a provider-backed action (chat, summarize, extract, translate, compare, expand a mind map, a writing action on a web page, and similar). When you do:

  • Your prompt, the selected model, the conversation context, and only the context you attached (page, file, Vault item, project, scrape result, transcript) are sent to the AI provider you configured.
  • Before sending, Basira shows you the provider, model, route, and an estimate of the context size, so you can review exactly what will be transmitted.
  • If you configure a local endpoint (e.g., Ollama running on your machine), requests go to that address and no cloud provider is involved.

Supported providers: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, and local/custom endpoints via Ollama or a base URL you set. Each provider processes your requests under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

A custom endpoint is any OpenAI-compatible service or local server whose address you save in Settings. Requests to it carry your prompt, the context you attached and the key you saved for that endpoint (if any), and go only to that address. Basira names the host before you save it and in the route line before you send.

The writing shortcut on web pages (Alt+B) sends the text you selected, or the text of the field you are in (up to 8,000 characters), to your configured provider when you pick an action in its panel, and not before. It does not read password fields.

A recording or audio file you transcribe is sent to OpenAI or Google Gemini when that is the provider you chat with (the dialog names it before you start); with any other provider it is transcribed on your device. Dictation in the chat is always transcribed on your device.

Other network requests Basira makes (none goes to us; we have no servers):

  • Watched pages. A page you chose to watch is requested again when you press "Check now" and on a timer (every 6 hours by default; you can change it or turn it off). Where the page can be read that way, the request goes to that page's own address without your cookies, so the site sees a visit without your sign-in, from your IP address. A page that can only be read where it is open (one that builds itself in the browser, or needs your sign-in) is read from the tab you already have open, as you see it there. The page text is kept on your device. It is sent to your AI provider only if you press "Sum up" and confirm, and then only the page's title and address, the changed lines and your note.
  • Smart Scrape "Add details from each page". After you confirm a dialog that names the site and the number of pages, Basira opens the pages your list links to on that same site, one at a time in a background tab, with your own sign-in on that site, as when you click them.
  • Web search and links. When you ask for a web search, your search words go to DuckDuckGo (or to Google, if you chose it; the other one is tried when the first gives nothing). Basira also reads the top result page, and what it found is sent to your AI provider together with your question. When you attach a link, Basira requests the linked page.
  • The browsing agent. When you start a task for it, it may search Google and open web pages it chooses in your tab, as you signed in, and follow links and paging there. Typing, submitting and other actions that change a page wait for your approval.
  • The list of models. When the app opens, it asks the provider you configured for its list of models, where that provider has one. That request carries your key for that provider and none of your content.
  • Model downloads. See "Security" below.

Third-Party Services

Basira itself uses no analytics platforms, no crash reporting, and no hosted fonts or scripts. Everything in the extension package runs locally. Three features reach a third party other than your AI provider, as described above: web search sends your search words to DuckDuckGo or Google, the browsing agent searches Google with words taken from the task you gave it, and model downloads come from Hugging Face.

The AI providers you optionally connect to are independent third parties. When you send a request, that provider receives the request content as described above.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent or license your data, and we receive none of it. Data you send to an AI provider goes only to that provider, only because you sent it, and only to answer your request. Search words you ask Basira to search for go to the search engine, and so do the browsing agent's searches in a task you started.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • All locally stored data lives in your browser profile and remains there until you delete it from within Basira or clear your browser data.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data from your browser.
  • You can delete individual chats, Vault items, projects, and prompts at any time inside the app, and export or back up your Vault before doing so.

Security

  • API keys are stored in your browser (local storage, or session storage if you keep them for the session only) and are sent only to the provider or endpoint address they belong to, over HTTPS, or over plain HTTP only to a server on your own device.
  • All JavaScript in the extension is bundled inside the package; no code is downloaded or executed from remote sources.
  • Optical character recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device.
  • Four optional features run AI models entirely on your device: semantic Vault search, on-device chat models, on-device text-to-speech models, and on-device speech to text (transcribing audio, and dictation in the chat). For each model you choose, the extension downloads its files (data, not code; between about 75 MB and about 1.3 GB depending on the model) once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you click its download button. That request, like any download, exposes your IP address to Hugging Face; it contains none of your content. Nothing is downloaded before you click, and what you search, write, save, say, or have read aloud is never sent anywhere by these features. Dictation puts the text in the message box; it is sent only if you press Send.
  • A key saved for a custom endpoint is stored together with that endpoint's address and is sent only to that address.

Children's Privacy

Basira does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all beyond what you type into your own AI requests.

Changes to This Policy

If Basira's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is published, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly. Because the extension has no accounts or email list, material changes will also be described in the release notes of the affected version.
